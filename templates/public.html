{% if page=='/watch-together' %}

A shared screen, wherever you are.

A watch party in a few steps

Create a room, invite someone and choose a supported video. Shared play, pause and seeking coordinate playback. Add videos to the queue to keep your evening going.

Conversation stays close

Room chat, pictures, GIFs and small gestures sit beside your video. Games run in separate screens without replacing your watch session.

Will every video play?

Availability, advertisements, autoplay, DRM and embedding restrictions belong to the source and browser. Solace cannot guarantee every YouTube video or protected subscription stream will play. Direct HTTPS MP4, WebM and browser-supported HLS sources can be used where compatible.

How to start a shared watch {% elif page=='/watch-rooms' %}

Make your own little space.

Private first

New rooms default to Private. Share a room code or invitation link with the people you choose. A host can regenerate the private link so old links stop working; existing room codes remain a separate way to join.

Deliberately public

Choose Public and confirm to appear in the app’s room directory. Only the room name, content label, capacity and participant count are listed. Hosts can return to Private without disconnecting participants.

Who manages a room?

The host can rename the room, change visibility, invite friends and moderate participants. Platform restrictions are managed separately by authorized staff.

Browse public rooms {% elif page=='/friends-messaging' %}

Keep your people close, with or without a stream.

Connect by choice

Copy your friend code, preview a profile and send a request. Sharing a room does not make someone a friend. Requests must be accepted before a private conversation can begin.

Messages that stay yours

Text, pictures and animated GIFs work independently of rooms. History persists for your identity; retrying a send does not create duplicate messages. Read receipts and friend-only presence have privacy controls.

What can others see?

Friends see only the profile fields you permit. Private conversations are authorized to participants and are not indexed by search engines. This is not end-to-end encrypted messaging; justified staff investigations may retain limited reported evidence.

Guests and recovery

A recognized guest can connect and message. Clearing browser data may lose access. Upgrading to an account preserves the current profile and connections.

Open your circle {% elif page=='/open-world' %}

A shared conversation. A little hello can go a long way.

A public community, separate from your room

Recognized guests and signed-in users can read the shared chat and choose to join the conversation. You do not need to become friends to post here. Pictures and animated GIFs use the same validated uploads as room chat.

Your choice, saved once

On first entry, choose to join or browse quietly. Solace remembers the introduction even if you navigate away. Joining shares your display name and messages with other community visitors. Online visibility and public profile fields can be changed in Settings, separately from friend-only sharing.

What does the online count mean?

It counts eligible, visible community visitors once across tabs. Hidden or disconnected tabs expire, and blocked or restricted identities are excluded. Quiet visitors are not counted.

Conversation and safety

Tap a name to preview authorized profile details, connect, block or report. Private messaging still requires accepted friendship. Chat content and online presence are not indexed by search engines. Community messages remain for up to 30 days or the newest 10,000 messages, whichever is reached first; deletion and reported evidence have separate handling in the privacy policy.

Guest profiles belong to this browser. Clearing browser data may remove access, and a new anonymous identity can evade an old identity restriction.

A little friendly play between scenes.

Three ways to play

Little Hearts & Stars brings a tic-tac-toe match, Love Hoops offers shared shots and scores, and Little Ludo brings turns, colors and a shared dice result. Rules are available before joining each game.

Your stream stays in place

Games use the room’s shared state. A game action does not replace the current video. Reduced-motion preferences soften effects; supported-device haptics can be disabled.

Can I play alone?

These games are designed for shared room play. Open a room and invite a friend before starting.

Be kind, respect boundaries

No harassment, abuse, hate, threats, sexual misconduct, impersonation, scams or spam. Respect a declined request. Do not distribute unlawful or non-consensual content, expose personal information or disrupt the service.

Block and report

Use profile and message reporting to flag content you can access. Explain what happened without adding unnecessary personal information. You can block immediately. Reports are not public, and report volume alone does not determine a penalty.

Review and appeals

Authorized staff may warn, mute, limit features, suspend or ban an identity, remove content or close a room. Restrictions include a reason and may be appealed from Safety & appeals. Staff investigate limited evidence; confidential investigation details are not disclosed to reporters.

Retention

Reported evidence is retained for up to 30 days during the normal review window, even if the original content is removed. Minimal case metadata and appeals are retained for 180 days. Audit records remain available to accountable staff; deployment backups follow the operator’s rotation policy.

For urgent personal danger, contact the relevant local emergency service. Solace is not an emergency response service.

A shared watch room

{% elif page=='/how-it-works' %}

A name, an invite, something to watch. That is enough to begin.

  1. Make a little room

    Create a room and choose your display name. Or join with a code someone shared. Keep invitations with people you trust: anyone holding the code can ask to join.

  2. Choose your first video

    Open Queue to search YouTube when search is configured, or add a supported HTTPS video URL. Availability depends on the source; choose another video if playback is restricted.

  3. Watch, talk, stay in step

    Everyone has play, pause and seek controls. Chat and games use separate screens, and a tap reveals controls in landscape. If a phone needs interaction for sound, tap Play.

  4. Keep what you want

    Create an account to retain your guest watch history on this browser. Your private history explains how viewing time is measured and can be cleared whenever you like.

{% elif page=='/privacy' %}

Private messages and pictures

Accepted friends can exchange private text, pictures and GIFs without a room. These records and restricted backups are stored on the server; they are not end-to-end encrypted. Private message bodies and attachments do not enter the operations chat archive or routine analytics. Read receipts require both friends to opt in. Blocking prevents conversation and media access. Removing your sent message clears its text and attachment for both participants; deleting from your inbox only hides your copy. Existing messages otherwise persist until removed or the sender deletes their account. Other participants keep their own messages. Guest chats depend on the browser identity and cannot be recovered after losing its browser data unless upgraded to an account.

Guest profiles, friends and room invitations

Guests can keep a name, picture and private watch history on the same browser. Clearing browser data can remove access; registering in that browser preserves the identity. Guest watch history follows the 30-day retention below. Profiles and connection preferences persist separately. Outside your explicit Open World sharing choices, only accepted friends can see an optionally shared picture, online status or aggregate activity. Detailed history and email addresses stay private. Recent watch companions are not automatically friends. You can remove connections, block people, hide sharing, remove your picture or delete history from your own screens.

New rooms are private. A host must deliberately make a room public before its name, content label and occupancy appear in discovery. A private code or invitation link grants access subject to bans, blocks and capacity. Hosts can revoke links. Friend invitations expire after 24 hours. Contact the operator about inaccessible guest data or other deletion requests.

Accounts and profiles

We store the email address you supply, a one-way password hash, display name, account creation date and optional profile picture. Profile pictures are re-encoded to remove original metadata and kept private to your account. Authentication uses essential, HTTP-only cookies and revocable server records. Account sessions expire after 30 days.

Rooms and chat

Display names and messages are visible to room participants. Room invitations use a shareable code, not an access list. A room is deleted when the final participant explicitly leaves; disconnected rooms have a reconnect grace period. A password-protected operations panel retains chat backups and operational records for up to 30 days, including after room deletion. This service is not end-to-end encrypted.

Private watch history

History stores the actual room playback instance, a source identifier without query tokens, dates, active viewing duration and companions' display names. Email addresses and sensitive participant identifiers are never shown in history. Viewing duration is credited only between consecutive visible, locally playing samples no more than 25 seconds apart, when room playback is also active. Pauses, buffering, background time and long disconnects are excluded. This is an estimate, not proof of attention.

Operational information

The server receives an IP address, browser user agent, pseudonymous browser identity, request timing, joins/leaves and activity events. Room clients report bounded playback, connection, viewport and error diagnostics. These help diagnose synchronization and abuse. An IP match does not establish a person's identity. These records are visible only to authenticated operations staff, with a 30-day retention target. Cleanup runs on requests and scheduled owner maintenance; dormant records may remain until cleanup executes. No location permission, contacts, microphone, camera, cross-site tracking or covert device fingerprinting is used.

Guests, storage and other services

Guests use essential cookies for a browser identity. Local storage keeps preferences and a display name. Guests can register without losing their current browser history. YouTube and directly linked video hosts receive playback requests under their own policies. Provider cookies or access rules may apply. Solace does not proxy those videos.

Your controls

Delete individual history entries, clear history, replace/remove a picture or delete an account from your private pages. Deleted entries do not reappear for the same current playback instance. Account deletion revokes sessions and removes the account, picture and owned history; companions see “Former participant.” Shared room messages and previously retained operational records are not silently rewritten by account deletion; contact the operator about those records. Server backups can retain data until the operator's documented rotation completes.

Open World community chat

Messages, names and explicitly shared profile fields are visible to recognized community visitors. Browsing quietly does not enroll you in its online count. Joining and visibility choices are stored with your profile and can be changed in Settings. Messages and public attachments remain for up to 30 days or the newest 10,000 messages, whichever comes first, with cleanup on use and scheduled maintenance. You can remove your own posts; account deletion clears owned public message content. Reports can retain a minimal snapshot separately.

Discovery and safety

Open World is opt-in and separate from friend-only presence. Public discovery never includes email addresses, IP addresses, precise activity times or private history. Reports retain a limited server-captured evidence snapshot for 30 days, including after original content deletion. Case metadata and appeals have a 180-day retention window. Named staff evidence access requires a reason and is audited. Routine operations logs exclude private message bodies; new room-chat archiving is disabled by default. Minimal identity-revocation records and staff action audits remain for security accountability until the operator archives the instance; ask the operator about its archive schedule.
